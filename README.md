# Privacy Policy for TailorPro

**Last updated: June 2026**

TailorPro ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the app.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

TailorPro uses authentication to allow users to securely access their data.

When you create an account or log in, we may collect:
- Email address (used for authentication and account identification)

When you make a purchase, we may collect:
- Purchase token (provided by Google Play to verify your subscription)
- Order ID (to identify and activate your subscription)
- Subscription status and expiry date (to manage your license access)

We do not collect payment card details. All payments are processed securely by Google Play.

We do not collect or process sensitive personal information beyond what is necessary for authentication, app functionality, and purchase verification.

---

## 2. How Your Data Is Used

Your information is used to:
- Create and manage your account
- Authenticate and identify you securely
- Associate your data (customers, orders, payments, etc.) with your account
- Verify and activate your Pro subscription
- Manage your license tier and access limits
- Provide core app functionality

---

## 3. Data Storage

Your data may be stored:
- Locally on your device, and/or
- Securely in your account via our backend service provider

Purchase verification data (order ID, purchase token, subscription status) is stored securely on our servers solely to manage your license access.

We do not access or use your data for purposes outside of providing the app's functionality.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

TailorPro uses the following third-party services:

**Supabase** — backend service provider for:
- Authentication
- Secure data storage and processing
- License management

Supabase may process your data in accordance with their own privacy policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

**Google Play Billing** — payment processing for Pro subscriptions:
- All payments are handled by Google Play
- We do not store your payment card details
- Google's privacy policy applies to payment processing: https://policies.google.com/privacy

**Google Play Android Developer API** — used to verify purchase tokens server-side to prevent fraud and unauthorized access.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

---

## 5. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

TailorPro offers a Pro subscription via Google Play Billing:

- The Pro plan is available as a **yearly auto-renewing subscription**
- Payment is charged to your Google Play account at confirmation of purchase
- Your subscription automatically renews each year unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date
- You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your **Google Play account settings**
- Refunds are handled by Google Play in accordance with their refund policy

When you purchase a subscription, we receive a purchase token from Google Play. This token is verified with Google's servers to confirm your payment and activate your Pro license. We store the order ID and subscription status to manage your access.

---

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not share your data except:
- When required to provide core app functionality through Supabase
- When verifying purchases with Google Play Developer API
- When you explicitly choose to share your data (e.g., exporting or sending files)

---

## 7. Backup and Export

TailorPro allows you to:
- Automatically sync your data to the cloud via your account
- Export your data as backup files
- Share files using third-party apps (e.g., WhatsApp, email, cloud storage)

These actions are fully controlled by you. We do not automatically transmit or share your data beyond what is required for sync and backup functionality.

---

## 8. Internet Usage

Internet access is required for:
- Account authentication
- Syncing or retrieving account-related data
- Verifying Pro subscription status

The app works offline for daily use. Subscription verification may fall back to a cached license status when offline, subject to a grace period.

---

## 9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, you are responsible for:
- Keeping your login credentials secure
- Protecting access to your device

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

---

## 10. Your Rights and Control

You can:
- Create and manage your account
- Access and manage your stored data within the app
- Export your data
- Cancel your Pro subscription at any time via Google Play
- Delete app data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage
- Request full account deletion (see below)

---

## 11. Children's Privacy

TailorPro is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
