# Privacy Policy for TailorPro

**Last updated:** 2026

TailorPro respects your privacy. This policy explains how your data is handled.

---

## Information We Collect

TailorPro uses authentication to allow users securely access their data.

When you create an account or log in, the following information may be collected:

* Email address (for authentication purposes)

---

## How Your Data Is Used

Your data is used to:

* Create and manage your account
* Securely associate your data with your account
* Enable app functionality (customers, orders, payments, etc.)

---

## Data Storage

* Your business data (customers, measurements, orders, payments) may be stored locally on your device and/or securely associated with your account.
* Authentication data is securely handled by our backend service provider.

---

## Third-Party Services

TailorPro uses **Supabase** as a backend service provider for:

* Authentication
* Secure data handling

Supabase may process your data in accordance with their privacy policy.

---

## Backup & Sharing

The app allows you to:

* Export your data as a backup file
* Share this file via apps such as WhatsApp, email, or cloud storage

These actions are initiated by you. TailorPro does not automatically share your data.

---

## Internet Usage

* Internet is required for account authentication and certain features.
* Core app functionality may still work offline depending on usage.

---

## Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data. However, you are responsible for:

* Keeping your login credentials secure
* Protecting your device

---

## Your Control

You have full control over your data, including:

* Creating and managing your account
* Exporting backups
* Deleting app data from your device

---

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page.
