# TailorPro Account Deletion Request

Last updated: April 2026

TailorPro is developed by Proxima Novum.

If you want to delete your TailorPro account and associated data, please follow the steps below.

## How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email to:

**peterduro2000@gmail.com**

Use the subject:

**Account Deletion Request**

In your message, include the email address used for your TailorPro account.

## Data That Will Be Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, the following data associated with your account may be deleted:

- Your TailorPro account
- Customer records
- Measurement records
- Order records
- Payment records
- App-related business data associated with your account

## Data That May Be Kept Temporarily

Some data may be retained temporarily where required for security, fraud prevention, legal, or record-keeping purposes.

## Deletion Timeframe

Account deletion requests will be processed within **7 days** of receiving the request.
